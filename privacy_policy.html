Privacy Policy

Titanium Cars — Last updated: July 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Titanium Cars ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our vehicle inventory management app.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name and login credentials, used to authenticate access to the App.
  • Location data: your device's location is recorded at the time you sign in, so administrators can confirm where a login occurred.
  • Photos and files: images you choose from your photo library, and spreadsheet or PDF files you upload to bulk-import vehicle inventory.
  • Inventory data: vehicle details entered or imported into the App, such as make, model, chassis/VIN number, registration, and related business records.
  • Usage data: basic activity within the App, such as which records were added, updated, or marked as sold, and by whom.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate users and control access to inventory data.
  • To record sign-in location for account security and audit purposes.
  • To let you add, import, view, and manage vehicle inventory records.
  • To generate dashboards and summaries of inventory activity.

Data Storage & Security

Login credentials are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage. Inventory and account data is transmitted securely to our servers and is accessible only to authorized users within your organization.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data collected in the App is shared only within your organization's account (e.g. between admins and staff using the same company account) and is not shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Your Choices

  • You can decline location or photo library access in your device settings; some features of the App may then be limited.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting us below.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use by dealership staff and administrators and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
