# Guideline 3.2 — Business: Response

## 1. Is the app restricted to users who are part of a single company or organization?

No. The app is publicly downloadable and includes a "Continue as guest" option on the
login screen. Any member of the public can tap this and immediately browse the current
vehicle inventory — no invitation, pre-approval, or affiliation with Titanium Cars is
required. Sensitive stock data (chassis/VIN numbers) is hidden from guest users; guests
see only vehicle name, make, status, and location.

Sign-in with an email/password account is only required for the internal
inventory-management features (adding/editing vehicles, marking vehicles sold, managing
staff, managing outlet locations). Those accounts are provisioned by an administrator for
Titanium Cars employees — but the app as a whole is not gated behind that login.

## 2. Is the app designed for use by a limited or specific group of companies or organizations?

No. The app is not a multi-tenant business tool used by a defined set of client
companies. It is a single dealership's (Titanium Cars) consumer-facing app: the public
browsing feature is open to anyone interested in viewing available vehicles, not
restricted to any partner or client organization. It is not possible for another company
to "become a client" of this app, because the app isn't structured as a B2B/SaaS product
— it is one business's own public app, with an internal staff-management layer built in.

## 3. What features in the app, if any, are intended for use by the general public?

- Guest Mode ("Continue as guest" on the login screen, no account needed):
  - Browse the full current vehicle inventory
  - Search vehicles
  - View vehicle details: make, model, status (active/sold), and outlet location
  - No invitation, registration, or organizational affiliation required — available to
    anyone who downloads the app

Chassis/VIN numbers and inventory-management actions (add, edit, delete, mark sold,
manage users/locations) remain restricted to authenticated Titanium Cars staff accounts,
since that data and those actions are internal operations, not public-facing content.

## 4. How do users obtain an account?

General public users don't need an account at all — guest access is immediate via
"Continue as guest." Staff accounts (Admin / Sales Person roles) are created by a
Titanium Cars administrator inside the app's Users management screen; there is no public
self-registration for staff accounts, since those grant access to internal business
operations.

## 5. Is there any paid content in the app, and if so, who pays for it?

No. The app is free to download and use, for both guest and staff users. There is no
in-app purchase, subscription, or other paid content.
